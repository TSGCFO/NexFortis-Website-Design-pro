## What NexFortis web design services include

When good web design happens, it is the result of a perfect harmony between the brand, message and conversion goals of a company. A fast website with clear content that works on your smartphone and ultimately guides users to where you want them to go is way more valuable than a pretty website that doesn’t do anything for your business. First impressions are largely based on the visual experience that users have with a company’s website, with 75% of users making a judgment about a company’s credibility based on the design of their website ([Stanford Web Credibility Research](https://www.kinesisinc.com/the-truth-about-web-design/)). But design alone is not enough. If the website does not perform, then all is lost. On mobile, 53% of users abandon a page that takes 3 seconds or longer to load ([Google](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/google-53-of-mobile-users-abandon-sites-that-take-over-3-seconds-to-load/426070/)). Unfortunately, fewer than half of mobile sites even score well on all three of the Core Web Vitals ([Web Almanac, 2025](https://almanac.httparchive.org/en/2025/performance)). Decoration vs. growth.

Our work at the web design services level covers the full build out for the site including strategy, user experience, and visual design. Our web design and development work ensures the site is fast, accessible, search engine friendly, and is based off of the real behavior of real users. As a Canadian company, with an office in the Greater Toronto Area, we work with clients from all over Ontario and the rest of Canada and design sites for Canadians and the Canadian shopping experience.
