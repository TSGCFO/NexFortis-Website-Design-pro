## What NexFortis web design services include

Good web design is where your brand, your message, and your conversion goals meet. A website that loads fast, reads clearly on a phone, and guides visitors toward a next step does far more for your business than one that only looks polished. First impressions are overwhelmingly visual — about 75% of users judge a company's credibility on its website design ([Stanford Web Credibility Research](https://www.kinesisinc.com/the-truth-about-web-design/)) — but design only pays off if the site performs: 53% of mobile visits are abandoned when a page takes longer than three seconds to load ([Google](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/google-53-of-mobile-users-abandon-sites-that-take-over-3-seconds-to-load/426070/)), and fewer than half of mobile sites pass all three Core Web Vitals ([Web Almanac, 2025](https://almanac.httparchive.org/en/2025/performance)). That is the difference between web design as decoration and web design as a growth tool.

Our web design services cover the full build — strategy, user experience, visual design, and **web design and development** — so the site that goes live is fast, accessible, search-ready, and built around how your customers actually behave. Because we are a Canadian-based company that knows the Greater Toronto Area and works with businesses across Ontario and the rest of Canada, we design for Canadian audiences and the way they buy.
