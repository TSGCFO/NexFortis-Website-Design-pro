## What NexFortis local SEO services include

Local SEO is the work of earning visibility in the searches that have local intent — "near me" queries, city-specific terms, and the Google Map Pack that sits above the regular results. That intent is enormous: roughly 46% of all Google searches have local intent ([BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/resources/local-seo-statistics/)), and 76% of people who search for something nearby visit a business within a day ([Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/local-seo-guide)). For most local businesses, that visibility is where the leads are: people searching for a nearby provider are often ready to call or visit.

A complete local SEO service is more than a one-time Google Business Profile tweak. ## Our local SEO services

We deliver the full range of local SEO services under one roof:

- **Google Business Profile optimization and management.** Your Google Business Profile is the single most influential asset in local search. Our Google Business Profile services cover initial setup, category selection, and complete optimization — business description, service areas, attributes, photos, Q&A, and Google Posts — followed by ongoing Google Business Profile management so it stays accurate, active, and aligned with how Google ranks local results.
- **Local citations and NAP consistency.** We audit your existing citations, correct inconsistent Name, Address, and Phone details across directories and data aggregators, and build authoritative new listings — reinforcing your legitimacy to Google and reducing the conflicting-data issues that suppress rankings.
- **Google Map Pack and local rankings.** We build and execute strategies aimed squarely at the Map Pack — the top three local results — through profile signals, citation authority, review velocity, on-page local relevance, and proximity factors, and we track your rankings across the areas you serve.
- **Reviews and reputation building.** Reviews are both a ranking signal and a conversion driver — 71% of consumers use Google to read local business reviews ([BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)). We implement compliant review-generation strategies and help you monitor and respond, working alongside our [reputation management](/services/digital-marketing/reputation-management) services where a deeper strategy is needed.
- **Near-me and local keyword optimization.** We research the exact local terms your customers use, then optimize your service and location pages, metadata, schema, and internal linking so your site communicates clear geographic relevance — the on-page foundation behind strong local rankings.
