## Findings

1. **The "local seo" cluster is strong and on-intent.** Six keywords, all confirmed **commercial + local** by Ahrefs, with good volume and low difficulty (KD 0–23). This is the page's core; primary `local seo services` confirmed.

2. **The 3 GBP keywords lean informational/branded, not commercial.** Ahrefs labels GBP optimization/management as informational + branded (gbp management KD 65). The fold-in still makes sense (local-SEO buyers want their GBP handled), but the page should **lead with the commercial local-SEO angle** and treat GBP as a managed-service sub-section + informational coverage, not a primary commercial target.

3. **Huge low-KD demand for `local seo [city]`** — Toronto 1000 (KD3), Vancouver 700, Edmonton 700 (KD2), Calgary 450, Markham 500, Uxbridge 400, Stouffville 700 (KD0)… This is the **geo layer (Phase B: [service]+[city] pages)**, correctly NOT in the national hub bucket. Strong real demand confirming the geo-as-primary decision.

4. **Broad `local seo` (1900, KD82) and `seo local` (600, KD80) are informational + hard** → correctly excluded; the page targets the commercial variants instead.

5. **Volume divergence** DfS vs Ahrefs ≈ 2–4× (normal; both agree directionally).
