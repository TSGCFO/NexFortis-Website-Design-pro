## What NexFortis link building services include

Building links from other good websites to yours is a big part of getting your site to show up higher in search results. These links are still one of the main ways Google decides how good a site is - if a page gets links from trusted sites, it will usually rank higher than a page that doesn't, all things being equal. For example, one study looked at 11.8 million search results and found that the top result had almost 4 times more links back to it than the pages that ranked 2-10. This is a big deal for businesses in Canada that are trying to stand out in a crowded market - having a strong set of links can be the difference between being on page one or page three of search results. But here's the thing: most pages don't have any links at all - 66.31% of them, to be exact, according to a study of 1 billion pages. That's a big opportunity for a business that wants to get ahead, and it's exactly what a focused link-building campaign can help with. You can think of link building like a vote of confidence from other websites - when they link to you, it's like they're saying "yeah, this site is good, you should check it out". And Google takes those votes into account when it's deciding how to rank sites. So, if you can get a lot of trusted sites to link to you, it can make a big difference in how visible your site is. It's not always easy, though - building links takes time and effort. But for businesses that are serious about getting ahead in search results, it's worth it. And that's where a focused campaign comes in - by targeting the right sites and building relationships with them, you can start to build up a strong set of links that will help you outrank your competitors.

A genuine link building service is far more than buying a batch of links. The links that help — and don't get you penalized — are editorial, relevant, and earned, which is why our approach centres on white-hat methods: real outreach to real publishers, content worth linking to, and digital PR that earns coverage. That focus reflects where the industry has landed: Aira's State of Link Building Report, the most comprehensive annual survey in the field, finds that content-led link building remains the most popular and effective tactic among SEO professionals ([Aira](https://aira.net/state-of-link-building/)). A complete link building service also means knowing what you already have, so we begin with a backlink audit and toxic-link disavow before we build anything new.

Because we are a Canadian-based company that knows the Greater Toronto Area and serves businesses across Ontario and the rest of Canada, we understand the publishers, industry sites, and local opportunities that carry weight for businesses here — not a generic list of links sold to everyone.
