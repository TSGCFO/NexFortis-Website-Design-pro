## What NexFortis link building services include

Link building is the work of earning links from other reputable websites back to yours, and those links remain one of the strongest signals Google uses to judge authority: a page that earns relevant, trusted links tends to outrank a page that hasn't, all else being equal. In one analysis of 11.8 million search results, the #1 result in Google had an average of 3.8x more backlinks than the pages ranking #2 through #10 ([Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/search-engine-ranking)). For most Canadian businesses competing in a crowded search market, a strong link profile is the difference between page one and page three — yet 66.31% of all pages have zero backlinks at all, based on a study of one billion pages in Ahrefs' Content Explorer ([Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/over-65-of-web-pages-have-no-links-31134.html)). That gap is exactly the opportunity a focused campaign is built to close.

A genuine link building service is far more than buying a batch of links. The links that help — and don't get you penalized — are editorial, relevant, and earned, which is why our approach centres on white-hat methods: real outreach to real publishers, content worth linking to, and digital PR that earns coverage. That focus reflects where the industry has landed: Aira's State of Link Building Report, the most comprehensive annual survey in the field, finds that content-led link building remains the most popular and effective tactic among SEO professionals ([Aira](https://aira.net/state-of-link-building/)). A complete link building service also means knowing what you already have, so we begin with a backlink audit and toxic-link disavow before we build anything new.

Because we are a Canadian-based company that knows the Greater Toronto Area and serves businesses across Ontario and the rest of Canada, we understand the publishers, industry sites, and local opportunities that carry weight for businesses here — not a generic list of links sold to everyone.
