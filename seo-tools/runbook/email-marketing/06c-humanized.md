We deliver the full range of email marketing services under one roof:

- **Email campaign management.** We plan, write, design, build, schedule, and send your campaigns — promotions, announcements, and seasonal pushes — then report on what each one earned, so every send has a purpose.
- **Email marketing automation and lifecycle flows.** This is where email earns its keep — automated campaigns see, on average, a 75% higher open rate and a 114% higher click-through rate than manual sends ([EmailTooltester](https://www.emailtooltester.com/en/blog/email-marketing-roi/)). Every one of these is also available as a focused engagement, and **small business email marketing** is something we deliberately scope to fit a smaller list and budget — so you are never paying enterprise rates to do email properly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much do email marketing services cost?** Email marketing pricing varies by list size, the number of automation flows and campaigns, your platform, and scope — which is why we scope every engagement after a free audit rather than quoting a one-size-fits-all price. We structure plans to be accessible for small businesses and scalable as your list grows.

**What is the best email marketing service?** The best service for your business depends on your list size, your goals, and whether you sell online or generate leads — there is no single right answer for everyone. We are platform-agnostic: in the free audit we assess what you use today and recommend the right fit.

**How much does it cost to send 10,000 emails?** Sending cost depends almost entirely on your email platform's pricing, which is usually tied to your number of subscribers rather than charged per send. The bigger driver of value is not the cost to send but the quality of the list, the segmentation, and the automation behind each send.

**How much is a 1000 email list worth?** A list's value is not fixed — it depends on how engaged, well-segmented, and deliverable it is, and on how well your campaigns convert it. A small, engaged list of buyers can outperform a large, neglected one, which is what list building and segmentation improve.

**What is the 80/20 rule in email marketing?** A common guideline is to keep roughly 80% of your email genuinely useful to the reader — helpful content and relevant offers — and reserve about 20% for direct selling. We build campaign calendars around that balance so your audience stays engaged.

**Do you offer email marketing for small businesses?** Yes. We scope small business email marketing so the work — and the pricing — fits a smaller list and budget, focusing first on the automation and clean-up that deliver the fastest return.
