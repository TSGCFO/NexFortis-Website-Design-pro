## Our email marketing services

We deliver the full range of email marketing services under one roof:

- **Email campaign management.** We plan, write, design, build, schedule, and send your campaigns — promotions, announcements, and seasonal pushes — then report on what each one earned, so every send has a purpose.
- **Email marketing automation and lifecycle flows.** This is where email earns its keep — automated campaigns see, on average, a 75% higher open rate and a 114% higher click-through rate than manual sends ([EmailTooltester](https://www.emailtooltester.com/en/blog/email-marketing-roi/)). - **eCommerce email.** For online stores, we connect email to your store and catalogue — automated flows, product recommendations, and segmented campaigns built to recover carts and turn buyers into repeat customers.

Every one of these is also available as a focused engagement, and **small business email marketing** is something we deliberately scope to fit a smaller list and budget — so you are never paying enterprise rates to do email properly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Pricing for email marketing services Email Marketing?** The cost of Email Marketing campaigns depends on several factors, such as the number of contacts in your mailing list, the number of different automation flows and campaigns, the current email marketing platform you use and the scope of work of the respective email marketing project. After our free email marketing audit we define the scope of work for each single project in detail to offer a fair price for you. Our offers are designed to be affordable for small companies and scalable with your mailing list.

**What is the best email marketing service?** The best email service provider for your business depends on the size of your email list, the goals of your email marketing efforts, whether you are an online store or generate leads in any other way. There is no best service for every business. We are platform-agnostic: in our free email marketing audit we will first check which email service provider you are already using and then recommend the best alternative for you.

**How much does it cost to send 10,000 emails?** This really depends on your email platform’s pricing, which is usually charged based on the number of subscribers on your list, not on the number of emails you send. Therefore, the cost of sending 10,000 emails is not the key factor in determining the value of the send. The value of the send is derived from the quality of your list, the level of segmentation and the automation behind the send.

**How much is a 1000 email list worth?** The value of a list is variable. It depends on a number of factors including how engaged it is, how well it has been segmented, how deliverable it is and how well your campaigns convert it to sales. A small list of engaged buyers can be far more valuable than a large list of disinterested people that you have never emailed. Building such a list and segmenting it well are key.

**The 80/20 rule in email marketing**: This is a general guideline on how to mix useful content (80%) with direct sales (20%). We use this to construct our campaign calendar so that you stay relevant to your audience.

**Do you offer email marketing for small businesses?** Yes. We scope small business email marketing so the work — and the pricing — fits a smaller list and budget, focusing first on the automation and clean-up that deliver the fastest return.
