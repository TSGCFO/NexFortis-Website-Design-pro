## What NexFortis Google Ads and PPC services include

Pay-per-click advertising is the work of buying the right clicks — putting your business in front of people at the exact moment they search for what you sell, and paying only when they click. Done well, it is the fastest way to generate qualified leads, because the intent is already there: someone searching "emergency plumber" or "commercial accountant near me" is ready to act. That intent is exactly why paid search converts — for commercial-intent queries, 65% of the clicks go to paid ads rather than organic listings ([WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2023/02/24/digital-advertising)). And the payoff is well documented: Google's own economic analysis estimates businesses earn an average of $2 in profit for every $1 they spend on Google Ads ([Google Economic Impact](https://economicimpact.google/methodology/)). Done poorly, though, it is one of the easiest ways to waste money, which is why professional ppc management exists.

A complete Google Ads and PPC service is far more than launching a few ads. Google rewards relevance across your keywords, ad copy, bids, and the landing page the click arrives on — and it all has to be tied to accurate conversion tracking so you know what is working. NexFortis runs those moving parts as one connected system instead of isolated tactics. Our google ads services cover the full lifecycle — strategy, build, launch, and ongoing optimization — so your spend is always working toward measurable results.

Because we are a Canadian-based company that knows the Greater Toronto Area and serves businesses across Ontario and the rest of Canada, we understand how Canadian buyers search, what local competition looks like in your market, and how to structure campaigns for the regions you actually serve.
