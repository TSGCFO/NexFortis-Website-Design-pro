## What NexFortis social media marketing services include

Social media marketing involves creating and managing audience on platforms where customers are, publishing content in which customers are interested, and responding to customers and others who interact with it. In some cases, it is also used for paid social advertising to reach more people. There are now 5.79 billion social media user or ‘identity’ accounts in existence worldwide, representing two in three people on Earth ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/social-media-users)). Each week, on average, a user spends 18 hours and 36 minutes on social media ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-global-overview-report)). The key function of social media marketing is to keep a) keep a customer facing brand visible to people who are not yet ready to buy from the company and/or b) use social media to prompt action from customers who are already ready to buy from the company.

Our complete social media marketing service is more than posting your tweets and Facebook updates. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are social media marketing services?** They cover the strategy, content creation, community management, paid social advertising, and analytics needed to build and run your presence on platforms like Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and TikTok. NexFortis delivers all of it as one connected service, backed by monthly reporting.

**How much is Social Media Marketing?** The price for Social Media Marketing will vary greatly depending on the number of platforms you wish to have content on, how often you’d like to post, and whether or not you want to use paid social media ads (which would be on top of the package price and spent on the respective social media platforms). Therefore, we scope out each project after a no charge initial consultation. Any ad spend would be in addition to the package price and spent on the corresponding social media platforms.

**Is it worth paying for social media marketing?** Yes, for most businesses, when done with a strategy. Consistent on-brand content and active social media community management will over time increase visibility and start to build trust with your audience. Paid social media can then be used to increase results from this work quickly. It just needs to be worked in a planned and measured way as opposed to just throwing up odd posts here and there.

**How is a social media marketing agency different from doing it in-house?** An agency brings strategy, consistent execution and measurement to your social efforts which an overstretched in-house team may struggle to sustain. We can run your social completely or support an existing in-house team. In either case, a senior consultant will remain on your account end to end.

**Which social media platforms should my business be on?** It depends on where your customers are and what you sell. Rather than spreading you across every network, we focus on the platforms — Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or TikTok — that fit your audience, then expand when it makes sense.

**How long does social media marketing take to show results?** Social media marketing is a months-not-weeks discipline. Building an engaged audience takes consistency over time, so we set realistic expectations up front rather than promising a fixed number of followers or sales by a fixed date.
