## What NexFortis social media marketing services include

Social media marketing is the work of building an audience on the platforms your customers use, publishing content they value, engaging with the people who respond, and — where it makes sense — amplifying it with paid social advertising. The reach is enormous: there are now 5.79 billion social media user identities worldwide — more than two in three people on Earth ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/social-media-users)) — and the typical user spends an average of 18 hours and 36 minutes on social media each week ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-global-overview-report)). Done well, social media marketing does two jobs at once: it keeps your brand visible to people who are not ready to buy yet, and it drives action from those who are.

A complete social media marketing service is more than scheduling a few posts. It connects strategy, a consistent content stream, active community management, and measurement that ties activity back to business results — and NexFortis runs all of it as one connected service rather than isolated tasks. As a Canadian-based company that knows the Greater Toronto Area and serves businesses across Ontario and the rest of Canada, we understand how Canadian audiences engage on social — part of why business owners choose NexFortis over generic, far-removed social media marketing companies.

**What are social media marketing services?** They cover the strategy, content creation, community management, paid social advertising, and analytics needed to build and run your presence on platforms like Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and TikTok. NexFortis delivers all of it as one connected service, backed by monthly reporting.

**How much does social media marketing cost?** Pricing varies by the number of platforms, the volume of content, whether paid social is involved, and your goals — which is why we scope every engagement after a free consultation rather than quoting a one-size-fits-all price. **Is it worth paying for social media marketing?** For most businesses, yes — when it is done with a strategy. **How is a social media marketing agency different from doing it in-house?** An agency brings strategy, consistent execution, and measurement an overstretched in-house team often cannot sustain. NexFortis can run your social entirely or support an existing team — and either way, a senior consultant stays on your account end to end.

**Which social media platforms should my business be on?** It depends on where your customers are and what you sell. Rather than spreading you across every network, we focus on the platforms — Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or TikTok — that fit your audience, then expand when it makes sense.

**How long does social media marketing take to show results?** Social media marketing is a months-not-weeks discipline. Building an engaged audience takes consistency over time, so we set realistic expectations up front rather than promising a fixed number of followers or sales by a fixed date.
