## What NexFortis conversion rate optimization services include

Conversion rate optimization (CRO) is the discipline of maximizing the percentage of visitors to a web site that complete a goal such as buying, filling out a form, booking a call with you, or signing up for a newsletter. In other words, instead of focusing on getting more traffic, CRO focuses on maximizing the percentage of visits that convert for you. As a general rule, there is lots of room for improvement. On a sample of 41,000 landing pages and 464 million visits, the median conversion rate was 6.6% ([Unbounce, Q4 2024](https://unbounce.com/average-conversion-rates-landing-pages/)). That means that for most web sites, the vast majority of the traffic you paid for to visit your site never converts into a desired action. But by making small and tested changes to your web site, you can cause that percentage to rise dramatically.

A complete conversion rate optimization service is more than a few landing-page tweaks here and there. It’s a service that links together your analytics tool, your user behavior data, your testing program, and your page design in a feedback loop that can be worked through by a service like NexFortis. Rather than working through fixes one by one as separate exercises, a complete service enables you to see where your visitors are dropping off, why they might be hesitating over a conversion, and which of the changes you make will result in an increase in your numbers. The key is to then keep iterating on what you find. Ideally, CRO works best with enough traffic to be able to test against, so we also work to establish the reality of what your current volume is able to prove.

As a Canadian company servicing businesses within the Greater Toronto Area and across Ontario and the rest of Canada, we have vast knowledge on how the Canadian buyer behaves on a page. ## Our conversion rate optimization services

We deliver the full range of conversion rate optimization services under one roof:

- **Conversion audit and data analysis.** Every programme starts with the data. We dig into your analytics to find where visitors enter, stall, and abandon — quantifying the gap between the traffic you have and the conversions you are getting, so the work targets your biggest opportunities first.
- **A/B and multivariate testing.** We design, run, and measure controlled experiments — A/B tests and multivariate tests — so changes are validated against real visitor behaviour rather than opinion. A/B testing is the workhorse of the discipline, accounting for 67.6% of all experiments practitioners run ([Convert, 2026](https://www.convert.com/blog/a-b-testing/ab-testing-stats/)). Winners get rolled out; losers inform the next test.
- **Conversion-centred design.** We redesign pages, calls to action, and layouts around a single goal: making the next step obvious and frictionless. Every element earns its place by helping the visitor convert.
- **UX and funnel analysis.** We map the full path from arrival to conversion, find the steps that lose the most people, and remove the friction — confusing navigation, unclear messaging, overlong forms, slow pages — that quietly costs you customers.
- **Heatmaps and user-behaviour analytics.** Click maps, scroll maps, and session recordings show what visitors actually do — where they look, where they get stuck, and where they give up — turning guesses into evidence we can act on.
- **Personalization.** Where the data supports it, we tailor messaging and offers to different audiences and intents, so more visitors see the version most likely to convert them.
- **eCommerce CRO.** For online stores, we optimize product pages, cart, and checkout to reduce abandonment and lift completed purchases — connecting the on-page experience to the rest of your store. The opportunity is large: the average documented online cart abandonment rate is 70.22% ([Baymard Institute](https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate)), so recovering even a fraction of those near-purchases can move revenue more than chasing new traffic.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is conversion rate optimization?** Conversion rate optimization (CRO) is the practice of increasing the percentage of your website visitors to convert. This can mean anything from a purchase online or over the phone, to filling out a form, giving a call or signing up for a service or offer online. As opposed to trying to get more visitors to your website, CRO focuses on maximizing your return from the visitors you already have by using your website analytics and user behavior data and by testing different variations to determine what works best.

**Pricing for CRO** We charge CRO based on traffic, complexity of your funnel and scope of testing. As all our projects are scoped after a free audit (of your choice, e.g. focused audit or ongoing monthly project) we can’t quote a fixed price upfront. But you can start where you need to!

**What is A/B testing and how does it work?** A/B testing is a method to compare your original page (A) with a variation (B) of your page by sending part of your traffic to B and measuring the results. The page that brings the most conversions wins. This way you rely on visitor behavior instead of your personal preference for a specific version. We can test for example two headlines or two different call-to-action buttons.

**A lot of traffic is not necessary for CRO to be effective** While we recommend that enough traffic is in place to test and reliably measure the results of tests, there is great value to audit-driven fixes to individual pages as well as the use of qualitative tools such as heatmaps and session recordings to better understand the behavior of users on your site as well as funnel analysis. Also, our job is to clearly state out what your current volume of traffic can prove and to develop an appropriate plan for completing the recommended work.

**How long does it take to start seeing results from conversion rate optimization?** CRO is a months-not-weeks discipline. Some very quick fixes may yield some very quick gains in the beginning, but any sustainable gains are going to come from running a steady stream of tests. Therefore, we set expectations accordingly and work off of a months-long schedule rather than trying to promise fixed results by a fixed date.

**How does P0 differ from a web design agency?** A P1 measures and optimizes already existing pages to best possible conversion, by means of tracking and A/B testing for every single change, in detail and with measurable results. A web design agency focuses on creating a website from scratch. NexFortis is also a web design agency and connects the measures of P1 and P0 for design-related decisions.
